# Acme Tasks quick guide

Acme Tasks keeps your team's projects, tasks, and deadlines in one place.

In today's fast-paced digital world, teams need one list they can trust.

## Add a member

You need the Owner role on the project.

1. Open the project
2. Select **Share**.
3. Enter the person's email address.

- create a title
- Assign an owner,
- Set a due date
